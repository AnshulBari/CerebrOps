Frequently asked

What exactly is CerebrOps? ×

An AI-powered deployment-intelligence platform. It ingests one webhook per pipeline stage, stores every run, learns the normal shape of each metric, and when something drifts it flags the anomaly — and names the deploy most likely to have caused it.

How long does setup take? +

Under five minutes. Point your CI at one endpoint, run the container, and the store, detectors, Prometheus endpoint, and dashboard come up together — no accounts, no SaaS dependency.

Is any of this accessible? +

Fully self-hosted and API-first. Everything is readable: the v1 API, the Prometheus scrape target, and structured JSON logs. Your telemetry never leaves your cluster.

Can I use it with my product? +

Yes. POST pipeline events from GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins; read the v1 API; scrape /metrics-prom; and route anomaly pages to Slack with the runbook attached.
