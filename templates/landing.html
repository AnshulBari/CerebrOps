01

Webhooks, not adapters

Your CI posts one JSON event per stage. CerebrOps stores it, timestamps it, and turns it into a living record of every run.

02

Builds you can interrogate

Durations, branches, commits, authors, stages. Filter, search, and drill into any run without leaving the dashboard.

03

Tests as a signal, not a gate

Pass rates feed your health model. A flaky suite that used to be background noise becomes a detectable drift.

04

Deploys that explain themselves

When an anomaly fires, CerebrOps correlates it against recent deployments and names the likely culprit — with the commit.

AI anomaly detection
